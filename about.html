About Me

I work across product design, UX research, and product discovery to help teams understand what users need, where the business is trying to go, and how the product experience should respond.

My work cuts across fintech, logistics, procurement, edtech, healthcare, and digital platforms. Across these spaces, I’ve designed customer-facing products, internal tools, websites, dashboards, and mobile experiences.

I’m especially drawn to complex product spaces where the problem is not fully defined yet. I enjoy the research, the messy middle, the trade-offs, and the process of turning unclear ideas into usable product experiences.

I recently completed an MBA in Marketing, which has deepened how I think about product strategy, positioning, customer behavior, and the relationship between design and business growth.

I’m currently open to product design, UX strategy, and product discovery roles with teams building thoughtful digital products across B2B and B2C fintech, commerce, logistics, and consumer technology.
